Tenzen privacy policy

Effective 11 October 2026

Tenzen is a number puzzle game for Android. This page explains what the app does with information. The short version: Tenzen does not collect, send or share your personal information.

What stays on your phone

Your progress, scores, settings, saved games and mini-game results are stored only on your device, inside the app's own storage. We never receive them. You can erase them at any time with Settings, Reset progress, by clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app.

What Tenzen does not do

  • It has no accounts, sign-in or profiles.
  • It shows no ads and includes no advertising or analytics tools.
  • It does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.
  • It does not connect to the internet to send or receive data. The game works fully offline.
  • It does not make purchases in this version.

Sharing a board

If you tap a share button, Tenzen hands a short text (for example a board code) to the Android share sheet or your clipboard. You choose where it goes. Tenzen does not send it anywhere itself.

Device backups

If you have Android device backup switched on, Android may back up the app's saved data to your own Google account, and restore it when you set up a new phone. That is handled by Google under your own account settings, not by us. See Google's own policies for how it treats backups.

Children

Tenzen is a general puzzle game. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Tenzen changes how information is handled (for example by adding purchases), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
